Rules & Procedure

The gavel
The chair recognises, the chair rules. A point of procedural order is made in the form the chair has given.
Debate
Speeches are timed and cut off. Interventions are made from the floor between recognitions.
The caucus
Consultation in caucus is where the actual work of a resolution is done, and it is done in smaller numbers.
Points
Scored on clarity of position, drafting, and conduct. Not on speaking longest.
Decorum
Doors close. Phones down. Decorum is a scored category.
